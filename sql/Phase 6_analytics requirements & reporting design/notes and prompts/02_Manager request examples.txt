Each section from 6.1 to 6.5 will represent a different business reporting need.

For example:

Section

	

Example manager request

	

Possible business questions




6.1 Sales Performance

	

Prepare a monthly sales performance report.

	

What are total sales? How are sales changing monthly? How many orders are completed?




6.2 Profitability

	

Prepare a report showing which sales generate profit.

	

What is the profit margin? Which products generate the most profit?




6.3 Product Performance

	

Identify the products and categories driving sales.

	

Which products sell the most? Which categories generate the most revenue?




6.4 Customer & Sales Channel

	

Analyze customer purchasing behavior and sales channels.

	

Who are the most frequent customers? Which channels generate sales, if the data supports this?




6.5 Inventory Performance

	

Prepare an inventory status and movement report.

	

What stock is available? Which products need replenishment, if the data supports it?

Recommended structure

5 main reporting requests, one for each business area.

Multiple business questions under each request, depending on the manager's needs.

Relevant KPIs to answer those questions.

One or more report pages where appropriate, rather than forcing every request into a single dashboard.

These will be realistic business scenarios for your portfolio, not actual requests from a real manager. We'll also make sure that every question and KPI is supported by the available data or clearly document its limitations.

This approach will demonstrate how you translate several business needs into measurable analytics and reports.
